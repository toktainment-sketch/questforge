FAQ

Common pilot questions.

What if an answer is wrong?

Every answer is a draft. Low-confidence answers and legal or contractual questions are flagged for your team before submission.

How is this different from Vanta or Drata?

Those tools manage controls and trust-center evidence. QuestForgeAI focuses on the buyer's custom questionnaire format.

What formats are supported?

The current operator workflow supports XLSX, DOCX, PDF, and TXT. XLSX is strongest for preserving questionnaire structure.

Is this public self-service?

No. QuestForgeAI is currently a controlled concierge pilot with human QA and operator-only processing.
