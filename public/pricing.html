FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need to book a call before buying?

No. Pricing is public, checkout is online, and intake happens by email. If you prefer to ask something first, email hello@questforgeai.ai and you will get a written answer.

What if an answer is wrong?

Every answer is a draft. Low-confidence answers and legal or contractual questions are flagged for your team before submission, and each package includes a revision pass.

How is this different from Vanta or Drata?

Those platforms manage controls and trust-center evidence. QuestForgeAI answers the buyer's custom questionnaire, in the buyer's own format, without a platform migration.

What formats are supported?

XLSX, DOCX, PDF, and TXT. XLSX is strongest for preserving the buyer's questionnaire structure.

Is my payment secure?

Checkout is operated by Paddle as Merchant of Record on PCI DSS compliant infrastructure. Card details never touch our servers.
