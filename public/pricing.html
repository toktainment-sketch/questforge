Common Questions

What if the answers are wrong?

Every answer comes with a confidence score. Low-confidence answers and all legal/contractual questions are flagged for your team to review before submission. You're reviewing a draft, not blind-trusting AI output.

We already have Vanta / Drata. How is this different?

Vanta and Drata are great for continuous monitoring and publishing your trust center. But when a prospect sends a custom 400-question spreadsheet, they don't fill it in for you. That's exactly the gap QuestForge fills.

What file formats do you accept?

Excel (.xlsx), Word (.docx), PDF, and plain text for both questionnaires and your security documentation. Most SIG and CAIQ questionnaires come in Excel, that's our strongest format.

How do you handle our confidential security documents?

Your documents are encrypted in transit and at rest. Each client's data is isolated, never shared, never used for training. We execute NDAs on request before you upload anything. Documents are deleted 30 days after delivery.

Can I try it with one questionnaire before committing?

Yes. The Standard plan is pay-per-questionnaire, no commitment. Try one, see the output quality, then decide if you want ongoing service.
