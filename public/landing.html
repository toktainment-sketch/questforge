Security posture

Your documents are treated as the sensitive assets they are.

Security paperwork deserves better than a public processing box. QuestForgeAI runs a deliberately controlled pipeline, and we only publish claims we actually operate.

No public processing workspace

The document workspace is operator-only and access-controlled. Files selected there are read in the browser, not stored by the website host.

Encrypted in transit, raw files stay local

Only the text needed to draft answers moves over TLS to the AI provider. Raw source files and generated spreadsheets stay in the operator's browser. Copies shared with us by email are deleted within 30 days of delivery.

Never used for AI training

Your documents are used only to draft your answers. They are not used to train models, and card details never touch our systems. Paddle handles payment as Merchant of Record.
