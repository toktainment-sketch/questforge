Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

QuestForgeAI respects the confidentiality of the documents you provide for a questionnaire drafting engagement. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and how we handle retention.

1. What We Collect

  • Contact information, including name, email, company, and billing details provided during intake or purchase.
  • Questionnaires and supporting documentation you choose for processing in the private operator workspace.
  • Basic operational logs required to run and secure the service.

2. How We Use It

We use provided documents solely to generate the draft questionnaire responses and review package you requested. Contact information is used for delivery, support, billing, and engagement follow-up.

3. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your confidential documents to train public AI models.
  • We do not share your documents outside the providers and operator workflow needed to deliver the engagement.

4. Retention and Deletion

Files selected in the private operator workspace are read in your browser and are not stored on the website host. Generated spreadsheets are also created in the browser for direct download. If you separately provide engagement files to our human operator through an agreed transfer channel, those copies are deleted within 30 days of delivery, or sooner upon written request.

5. Security

QuestForgeAI is operated as a controlled, human-reviewed drafting service. Operator access is restricted and public processing access is disabled. Files are parsed locally in the browser. Only the text needed to draft answers is sent over TLS through Cloudflare to Anthropic's API; raw files are not uploaded to or stored on the hosting server.

6. Sub-processors

We use a short list of providers to operate the service: Cloudflare for website delivery and secure API routing, Anthropic for AI draft generation under commercial API terms with no training on inputs, and Paddle for payments, tax handling, and billing as Merchant of Record. These providers process data only as needed to deliver the service. The full description is on the Security overview.

7. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to hello@questforgeai.ai.
