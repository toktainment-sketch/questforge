Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

QuestForge ("we", "us") respects your privacy and the confidentiality of the documents you share with us. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

1. What We Collect

  • Account & contact information: name, email, company, provided when you contact us or purchase.
  • Documents you upload: security questionnaires and supporting documentation (e.g., policies, reports) you submit for processing.
  • Usage data: basic technical logs needed to operate the Service.

2. How We Use It

We use uploaded documents solely to generate the draft questionnaire responses you requested. We use contact information to deliver results and communicate with you about your engagement.

3. What We Do NOT Do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your confidential documents to train public or third-party AI models.
  • We do not share your documents with anyone outside the processing of your engagement.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your uploaded documents only as long as needed to deliver and support your engagement. Client documents are deleted within 30 days of delivery, or sooner upon written request to hello.questforgeai@gmail.com.

5. Security

Documents are transmitted over encrypted connections (TLS) and stored with access restricted to the personnel processing your engagement. Each client's data is kept isolated.

6. Sub-processors

We use a small number of trusted service providers to operate the Service, including a cloud hosting provider, an AI model provider for drafting, and a payment Merchant of Record. These providers process data only as needed to deliver the Service.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing us. We honor data deletion requests promptly.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: hello.questforgeai@gmail.com
