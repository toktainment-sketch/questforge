Refund Policy

Last updated: June 2026

We stand behind the quality of our work. This policy explains when refunds apply.

Our Quality Guarantee

For your first questionnaire engagement: if the delivered draft is not at least 80% complete against the questions answerable from the documentation you provided, you may request a full refund within 7 days of delivery. We would rather refund a dissatisfied first-time client than leave you unhappy.

How Refunds Work

  • Email hello.questforgeai@gmail.com within 7 days of delivery explaining the issue.
  • We will first offer to revise the work to meet the standard.
  • If we cannot, we issue a full refund through our payment provider, typically within 5–10 business days.

What Is Not Refundable

  • Engagements where the documentation provided was insufficient to answer the questionnaire, after we requested and did not receive the needed materials.
  • Completed monthly subscription periods already delivered.
  • Requests made more than 7 days after delivery.
