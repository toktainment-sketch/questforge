# QuestForgeAI Premium Sales Pack And Proof Assets

Updated: 2026-06-30

## Standard

The sales pack must make QuestForgeAI feel credible before a prospect speaks to
us. The buyer should understand the pain, see the workflow, trust the control
model, and believe the pilot is low-risk.

## Asset 1: One-Page PDF

Title:

```text
QuestForgeAI
Security Questionnaire First-Draft Service for B2B SaaS Teams
```

Sections:

- The bottleneck: custom buyer questionnaires slow enterprise deals.
- The workflow: questionnaire plus evidence docs becomes a reviewed draft.
- What the pilot includes: draft answers, confidence notes, legal/risk flags,
  cover note, human QA.
- What it is not: no auto-submit, no unsupported claims, no replacement for legal
  or security approval.
- Best-fit teams: B2B SaaS selling into enterprise, fintech, healthcare, HR,
  devtools, legaltech, govtech, or education.
- Pilot price: Standard USD 1,200; Large USD 1,800; Monthly USD 4,500.
- CTA: "Send one active questionnaire for a controlled pilot."

Visual direction:

- Mature blue from the KAMTOBE/QuestForgeAI identity.
- Clean tables, evidence cards, confidence flags.
- No cartoon AI graphics.
- No generic laptop stock imagery.

## Asset 2: Before/After Visual

Goal:

Show the transformation in one glance.

Before:

- Buyer spreadsheet with many unanswered rows.
- Evidence scattered across SOC 2, policies, privacy docs, prior answers.
- Sales/security/legal handoff confusion.

After:

- Draft answer column populated.
- Source/evidence note.
- Confidence rating.
- Legal/risk flag.
- "Needs client confirmation" items clearly marked.

Use synthetic data only. Do not use client documents.

## Asset 4: 60-90 Second No-Face Demo Video

Format:

- Brand-led screen recording or animated product walkthrough.
- Optional voiceover; captions must carry the message if no voice is used.
- Screen recording or animated product walkthrough.
- Clear before/after progression.
- Captions for silent LinkedIn viewing.

Script:

```text
Enterprise security questionnaires often arrive after the buyer is already
serious. The company may have the evidence: SOC 2, policies, privacy documents,
and previous answers. But the buyer wants it in their own spreadsheet.

QuestForgeAI turns that material into a review-ready first draft.

Here is the workflow: upload the questionnaire, add supporting documents, enter
the company and buyer names, and generate a draft package.

The output is not auto-submitted. It includes draft answers, confidence notes,
low-evidence flags, and legal or contractual questions that need human review.

The goal is simple: help sales move faster while keeping security and legal in
control of the final wording.

QuestForgeAI is opening a controlled concierge pilot for B2B SaaS teams with
active enterprise questionnaires.
```
