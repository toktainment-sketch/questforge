## Steering Prompt

Paste this into NotebookLM:

```text
Create a private preview video that helps me emotionally and visually understand
my LinkedIn comeback plan before I approve the final direction.

Use only the uploaded source material. Do not invent achievements, job titles,
companies, metrics, awards, or results.

The video is for me, Ifeoma Odumodu, so I can see whether the repositioning feels
right before we update LinkedIn publicly.

Core story:
My LinkedIn profile currently reflects an older professional chapter:
communications, stakeholder engagement, media relations, brand storytelling,
corporate messaging, ports/logistics, and corporate credibility.

The new chapter is KAMTOBE Creations: a serious digital product and AI workflow
company. QuestForgeAI is the first strong product signal under that umbrella.

This is not a loud influencer rebrand. It is a refined professional comeback.
The goal is for anyone who checks my profile to sense growth, discipline,
execution, and a clear new direction.

Video structure:
1. Opening: "A profile that still tells the old story."
2. Current state: credible communications background, real network, but no
   visible founder/product-builder signal yet.
3. The shift: from strategic communications to AI workflow and digital product
   building.
4. The new positioning: KAMTOBE Creations as the umbrella, QuestForgeAI as the
   first serious B2B product.
5. The LinkedIn upgrade: banner, headline, About section, current founder role,
   Featured assets, and comeback post.
6. The emotional outcome: refined, premium, intelligent, mature, and surprising
   in the best way.
7. Closing: "This is the comeback page before the market sees the comeback."

Tone:
Premium, calm, powerful, mature, elegant, strategic, confident, and understated.
Do not make it motivational hype. Do not make it too dramatic. Do not make it
look like a personal-brand influencer video.

Visual direction:
No face required.
Use abstract LinkedIn profile cards, clean dashboard scenes, refined document
layouts, workflow lines, polished product screens, and elegant before/after
transitions.

Show the old profile as credible but outdated.
Show the new profile as modern, focused, premium, and business-ready.

Correct naming:
Use KAMTOBE Creations exactly.
Use QuestForgeAI exactly.
Do not use Canva, Canterbury, Cantaberry, Cantobe, or any other variation.

Avoid:
- Fake achievements
- Fake client logos
- Fake team photos
- Fake founder headshots
- Cartoon AI imagery
- Overly flashy visuals
- Claims that QuestForgeAI is fully self-service or replaces legal/security review

Desired feeling:
After watching, I should be able to say:
"Yes, this reflects the level-up. This is the professional comeback direction I
want people to see."
```

## What To Review After NotebookLM Generates The Video

Check:

- Does the profile transformation feel premium?
- Does it preserve your communications credibility?
- Does it make KAMTOBE Creations feel serious?
- Does QuestForgeAI feel like a real product, not a vague idea?
- Does it avoid making you the face of every business?
- Is the tone mature enough for LinkedIn?
- Are all brand names spelled correctly?

If the answer is yes, proceed to LinkedIn profile edits.
