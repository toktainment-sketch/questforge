# QuestForgeAI Cold Outreach Sequence v1

Written: 2026-07-05

Aligned with the self-serve website: every CTA points to the pricing page, never to a call. Personalize the two bracketed fields only. Send from hello.questforgeai@gmail.com or hello@questforgeai.ai once verified.

Target: Heads of Sales, Sales Engineering leads, CISOs/security leads, and founders at 10-200 person B2B SaaS companies in the US, UK, Canada, and Australia that sell to mid-market or enterprise.

Trigger signals to prioritize (from LinkedIn/job boards):
- Hiring for "Sales Engineer" or "GRC Analyst"
- Posts complaining about vendor security reviews or questionnaires
- Recently announced an enterprise customer or moved upmarket
- SOC 2 announcement in the last 12 months (they have evidence, now they get questionnaires)

## Rules

- No fake urgency, no invented case studies, no percentages we cannot prove.
- Never attach files in cold email. Links only, to our own domain.
- One thread per prospect. Reply in thread, do not restart.
- Log every send in premium-prospect-tracker-template.csv.
- Stop immediately on any unsubscribe-style reply and record it.
