# QuestForge, Sales Call Script

**Call length:** 15-20 minutes
**Goal:** Understand their pain → show relevance → close on a paid pilot ($1,200)

---

## OPENING (2 minutes)

"Thanks for the time, {{firstName}}. I want to make sure this is actually relevant before I tell you anything about what we do.

Can I ask, when a prospect sends your team a security questionnaire, a SIG, a CAIQ, or a custom one, what happens next? Who owns it, how long does it take, and how much of your team's time does it consume?"

**Let them talk. Take notes. Don't interrupt.**

---

## DISCOVERY QUESTIONS (5-7 minutes)

Ask these based on the conversation flow, you don't need all of them:

1. "How many security questionnaires does your team receive per quarter?"
2. "What's your average turnaround time from receiving one to submitting it?"
3. "Has a deal ever stalled or died during security review? Do you know the approximate deal value?"
4. "Do you have a centralized answer library, or does your team start fresh each time?"
5. "Who typically owns this, security, sales engineering, or does it float between people?"
6. "What would it mean for your pipeline if you could turn questionnaires around in 24 hours instead of 3-4 weeks?"

**Listen for:**
- Number of questionnaires per quarter (3+ = strong fit)
- Deal values at stake ($50K+ ACV = budget is obvious)
- Who currently does the work (if CEO/CTO is doing it, urgency is high)
- Existing answer library (if none, pain is maximum)

---

## POSITIONING (3 minutes, only after discovery)

"Based on what you've described, here's what we do:

We take your security documentation, SOC 2 report, pen test, privacy policy, any existing answered questionnaires, and build a knowledge base from it.

Then when you get a new questionnaire, you upload it to QuestForge. Our AI reads every question, matches it against your documentation, and generates a complete draft response.

Every answer comes back with a confidence score, HIGH, MEDIUM, or LOW. Legal and contractual questions are flagged so your team knows exactly what to review before submitting.

You also get a professional cover letter ready to send to the prospect.

The whole thing takes hours, not weeks. First questionnaire is $1,200."

---

## HANDLING OBJECTIONS

### "What if the answers are wrong?"

"Great question, and this is the most important part. We don't auto-submit anything. Every answer comes back with a confidence score. Low-confidence answers are flagged for your team. Legal questions, anything about indemnification, SLAs, liability, are always flagged without exception.

Your team is reviewing a polished, 85% complete draft. They spend 30 minutes refining instead of 30 hours building from scratch."

---

### "We have Vanta / Drata."

"Vanta is great for continuous monitoring and your trust center, and I'd actually recommend keeping it. But when a prospect sends you a custom 400-question spreadsheet, Vanta doesn't fill it in for you.

Think of it this way: Vanta proves you're compliant. QuestForge communicates that compliance in the format the prospect requires. They solve different problems."

---

### "We have a security team that handles this."

"How many hours per questionnaire does it take them? And what would they rather be doing with those hours?

Most security teams we talk to spend 15-30 hours per questionnaire. With QuestForge, they spend 1-2 hours reviewing a draft instead. That frees them up to work on actual security, not paperwork."

---

### "That sounds expensive."

"$1,200 per questionnaire. If your average enterprise deal is $50K ACV, and one deal closes even one week faster because the questionnaire was done in 24 hours instead of 4 weeks, that $1,200 generated $50,000 in revenue.

And if you're doing 5+ per month, the monthly plan is $4,500, unlimited questionnaires. That's less than a week of a junior security analyst's time."

---

### "Can we see a demo first?"

"Absolutely, but the best demo is your own questionnaire. If you have one in your queue right now, give it to me with your security docs. I'll run it through QuestForge and you'll see the actual output on your actual questions. That's more convincing than any demo I could show you.

If you'd rather see a generic sample first, I can send that over today."

---

### "We need to think about it."

"Totally understand. Let me ask this, do you have a questionnaire sitting in someone's inbox right now? Because if you do, let me run it. If the output isn't good enough, you don't pay. Zero risk.

The worst case is you get a free draft of something that was going to take someone 3 weeks anyway."

---

## CLOSE (2 minutes)

**Primary close, pilot:**
"The simplest next step: you send me your next questionnaire and your security docs. We run it through QuestForge and deliver the draft within 24 hours. You see the output quality on your own questions. $1,200, and if it's not at least 80% complete, you don't pay. Does that work?"

**If they need time:**
"No problem. Can I send you a sample output today so you can see what a completed questionnaire looks like? And when your next one comes in, you'll know where to send it."

**If they're ready now:**
"Great. I'll send you a link to upload your security docs and the questionnaire right after this call. You'll have the completed draft by tomorrow. What email should I send the upload link to?"

---

## POST-CALL

1. Send follow-up email within 1 hour with upload link
2. If they committed to a pilot, send Stripe payment link + upload instructions
3. If they asked for a sample, send the sample output PDF
4. Add to CRM with notes: questionnaire volume, deal sizes, current process, timeline
5. Follow up in 3 days if no upload received
