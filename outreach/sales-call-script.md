# QuestForge Sales Call Script

**Call length:** 15-20 minutes
**Goal:** Qualify the pain, explain the controlled pilot, close on one paid
questionnaire engagement.

## Opening

"Thanks for the time, {{firstName}}. Before I describe QuestForge, I want to
understand whether this is actually painful for you.

When a prospect sends a SIG, CAIQ, or custom security questionnaire, what happens
next? Who owns it, how long does it take, and where does it usually get stuck?"

## Discovery Questions

- How many security questionnaires do you receive per quarter?
- What is the average turnaround from receipt to submission?
- Who currently drafts answers?
- Do you have a reusable answer library?
- What deal value is usually attached to these questionnaires?
- Which questions create the most risk: technical, legal, privacy, or SLA?
- Do you have a questionnaire in progress right now?

## Positioning

"QuestForge is a concierge pilot for security questionnaire first drafts.

You provide the questionnaire and supporting security documentation. We run the
documents through an AI-assisted drafting workflow, then a human operator reviews
the output before delivery.

The package includes a draft questionnaire response, confidence notes, legal and
low-evidence flags, and a cover note. Your team still approves the final wording
before submitting to the prospect.

The first pilot starts at $1,200."

## Objections

### "What if the answers are wrong?"

"That is exactly why this is not an auto-submit tool. The output is a draft.
Low-confidence answers and legal or contractual questions are flagged, and your
team approves final wording before anything goes to the buyer."

### "We have Vanta / Drata."

"Keep it. Those tools are useful for controls and trust centers. QuestForge is
for the custom spreadsheet or questionnaire a buyer sends when they want answers
in their own format."

### "Our security team handles this."

"That may still be the right owner. QuestForge is meant to reduce the blank-page
work so your security team reviews and corrects a structured draft instead of
building everything from scratch."

### "That sounds expensive."

"The question is usually whether a faster, cleaner draft is worth $1,200 against
the value of the enterprise deal and the hours of senior team time involved. A
single delayed deal often costs more than the pilot."

### "Can we see a demo first?"

"Yes. I can send a sample output. The strongest demo is one of your real
questionnaires, but we can start with a sample if that is easier."

## Close

"The simplest next step is one controlled pilot. You send the questionnaire and
supporting docs through the agreed channel, we return a reviewed draft package
within the agreed turnaround, and your team decides whether the workflow is worth
using again. Does that make sense for the next questionnaire in your queue?"

## Post-Call

1. Send recap within 1 hour.
2. Confirm payment path or pilot exception.
3. Confirm NDA and file-transfer method.
4. Log expected delivery deadline.
5. Follow the concierge pilot SOP.
