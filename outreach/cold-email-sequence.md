## Personalization Notes

- Reference the prospect's company and buyer segment.
- Mention their Trust Center only if you actually reviewed it.
- If they recently raised funding or moved upmarket, connect that to increased
  enterprise security review volume.
- Do not promise fixed completion percentages, public uploads, or compliance
  controls that are not implemented.
